## What this bundle changes (5 files, `ai-smart-schedule` only; base `dea77755`)
1. `harness/evidence-pack.ts` — conditional rules become scouting watch-briefs, not dated cards (from the earlier legibility fix); HARVEST/POST_HARVEST rules anchor to the last stage they name.
2. `harness/index.ts` — folds watch-briefs into the stage's scouting card; folds same-stage irrigation / harvest / post-harvest rule cards into the baseline card of that type (one card per stage per type).
3. `db/agronomy-repo.ts` — SSP-class products qualify as product equivalents.
4. `harness/llm-candidates.ts` — the agronomist brief: universal calendar pattern as the model's checklist, farmer-simplicity rules (one card = one job; water as a visible field condition with an interval; inputs as what to buy and how to mix), explicit free hand to propose what the crop and method need, with every number still gated; method-mismatch advisory when the DB plan is for another method.
5. `generator/narrate.ts` — extension-officer register by meaning, no language-specific words; numbers/units/grades untouched.
